# TU B.Sc. Third Year syllabus source notes

The initial academic outline is based on the Tribhuvan University, Institute of Science and Technology, *4 year's Bachelors of Science Revised Course of Study-2073, Third & Fourth Year* PDF:

- Official TU PDF: https://portal.tu.edu.np/downloads/syllabus34136_2023_05_10_06_50_22.pdf
- The PDF identifies the Third Year subjects and contains the Botany, Chemistry and Research Methodology theory course descriptions.
- Relevant paper metadata in the PDF: BOT 301 Plant Biochemistry and Biotechnology (theory, 100 marks); CHE 301 General Chemistry I (major theory, 100 marks); CHE 303 Basic Biochemistry (elective theory, 50 marks); BOT 304 Medicinal and Aromatic Plants (theory, 50 marks); RM 305 Research Methodology (compulsory theory, 100 marks).
- TU's 2082/2083 B.Sc. examination notice also lists RM 305, CHEM 301, BOT 301, BOT 304 and CHEM 303, corroborating the paper codes and titles: https://portal.tu.edu.np/notice/11674/1773747875.pdf

The seed command creates a concise, editable topic index for each official unit/group. It does not reproduce the full syllabus prose, suggested readings, or every sub-clause. The original TU PDF remains the authority; students should compare their admission-batch copy and campus notices if TU has issued a later revision. Practical papers are intentionally not seeded, per the app owner's requirement.
